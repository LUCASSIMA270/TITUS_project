# TITUS_project

🌊 TITUS Project: Coastal Development in the Var Region
Business Settlement, Economic Dynamism, and Environmental Preservation

This repository contains the final report and analysis for the Tutorized Project (TITUS), which investigates the impact of business locations along the Var coastline. The study examines how urban planning can balance the economic growth of the Toulon metropolitan area with the protection of fragile ecosystems in the French Riviera (PACA region).
🧐 Research Question

The core objective is to evaluate how the planning and geographic placement of businesses impact the economic and environmental development of the coastline. The report analyzes the repercussions of this spatial configuration on the region's vitality and the health of its ecosystems.
🛠️ Methodological Framework

    Case Study: A focused analysis on the city of Toulon, the economic capital of the Var department.

    Key Indicators: Tracking trends in employment (via INSEE), local taxation, and tourism revenue.

    Field Research: Qualitative interviews with the Director of the "Technopole de la Mer" and founders of local startups (e.g., Green Score Capital).

    Sustainable Urbanism: Applying concepts like ZAN (Zero Net Artificialization) and PLUI (Intercommunal Local Urban Plan) for responsible land management.

📂 Repository Contents

    PROJET TUTORE.pdf: The full report, including an analysis of dominant sectors such as viticulture, naval industry, and renewable energy.

    appendices/: Public opinion surveys and transcripts of expert interviews.

    visualizations/: Charts showing the decrease in Toulon's unemployment rate (from 10.1% to 6.4% over ten years) and the reduction of public debt per capita.

📈 Key Findings & Perspectives

    Economic Dynamism: A 46% increase in the number of businesses in Toulon over a decade, driven by an attractive and stable tax policy.

    Technopole de la Mer: The leading European hub dedicated to maritime safety and security, serving as a catalyst for innovation and the regional "Blue Economy."

    Energy Transition: Development of a "zero-emission" seawater thermal energy network and new projects for biomethane production.

    Urban Reinvention: The Port Marchand/Pipady project, aimed at opening 90,000 m² to the sea with eco-exemplary buildings and a 20,000 m² green park.

🚀 Strategic Recommendations

    Supporting Startups: Creating incubators specifically for green tech and circular economy solutions.

    Decarbonized Mobility: Improving communication regarding electric bikes and modernizing public transport networks.

    Biodiversity Protection: Implementing ecological corridors and strictly protecting surrounding mountain ranges (Faron, Baou).

    Sustainable Tourism: Promoting ecotourism to mitigate the impact of seasonal overpopulation.
